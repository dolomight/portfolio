Exal Visualizer

Exal · Sales visualiser

A configurator that puts the customer's own brand on the bottle before the sales meeting starts.

Exal makes aluminium bottles and cans for the world's beauty, beverage and household brands, and its sales team needed a faster way to show a prospect what their product would look like in Exal packaging. The old answer was a mock-up from the studio and a week's wait.

The Visualizer moved that into the browser. From a secure admin, a rep configures a set of logos, colours and decorations for each target customer, then shares a link. The prospect turns the bottle, tries their brands on it and, at the end of the session, receives a generated PDF of every configuration by email. Nothing is rendered in advance; every meeting starts with the customer's brand already on the shelf.
