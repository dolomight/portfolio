QN2A · Stand Up To Cancer

Stand Up To Cancer · QN2A

A place for cancer patients to share the questions they wish they'd asked, built for a national campaign with James Denton.

QN2A was the destination site for a national Stand Up To Cancer media campaign fronted by actor James Denton. Its premise was simple and generous: patients and caregivers submit the questions they wish they had known to ask at the start of their cancer journey, so the next person does not have to learn them the hard way.

The site ran as an infinite scroll with the newest submission on top. Visitors could favourite questions, share them, and see the community's top-ranked list; favourites could be printed to take to a physician appointment. Inappropriate posts could be flagged for admin review. It lived for the length of the paid campaign and did exactly what it was built to do.
